# Investigate

Answer the repository question from read-only evidence. Establish what would confirm or disprove the explanation,
follow relevant code/tests/history, and distinguish observed facts from inference. Use the investigation workflow.
Only an explicit request to save a delivery plan permits its one-new-plan exception; investigation never implements
or approves the proposed change.
